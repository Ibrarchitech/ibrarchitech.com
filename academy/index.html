THE GAP

Saudi Arabia has no hub dedicated to cinematic arts, where every craft learns together, forms crews and shows its work.

SOURCES: STATISTA (2019) · BRITISH COUNCIL, SAUDI FILM SKILLS (2019) · FILM COMMISSION STRATEGY (2021)

■ WHERE THE MODEL EXISTS

Comparable schools exist abroad. In Saudi Arabia and the Gulf, not yet.

The model: dedicated to film, open to newcomers, the crafts trained together, its own production building, and a recognised qualification.

  • AFI ConservatoryLos Angeles, USA
  • New York Film AcademyNew York, USA

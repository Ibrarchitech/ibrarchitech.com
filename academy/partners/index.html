FOR PARTNERS · PROJECT BRIEF

A model for a film academy in Jeddah, built on evidence.

This graduation project studies what a hub for cinematic arts in Jeddah would need: who it serves, what it teaches, how many it holds and what the building must do. The research is open. Partners are welcome to review it, add to it and use it.
